# LOAM research inheritance closure

Status: **repository-level coverage checkpoint**

This checkpoint asks one narrow question:

> Has HRA-N preserved the durable value of LOAM's research program without
> turning the LOAM repository into a permanent runtime, source-layout, or
> document-by-document dependency?

It is not a claim of feature parity, proof parity, or research completion.

## Scope

Coverage review baseline:

- LOAM: `8b26bfe8cb953d2543879cbe93570d020d2c7f92`
- HRA-N: `fafdf036eb6f5c730369a4649950b36e4b6df2c6`
- LOAM `docs/research`: 154 files enumerated at tree
  `8e0a91db1371e57790b08808bf48c2143699d644`

The review enumerated the complete research tree and inspected the current
authority/index documents and representative records for each family. It does
not assert that every historical research file was reread line-by-line. The
closure unit is the **research family and durable law**, not the individual note.

## Disposition vocabulary

| Disposition | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **DISTILLED** | Durable law, pressure, or method is stated in current HRA-N design authority. |
| **HRA-N NATIVE** | The question is inherited, but Ada/SPARK must re-establish the answer independently. |
| **HISTORICAL** | Useful evidence for archaeology or regression diagnosis; no permanent HRA-N copy is required. |
| **LOAM-SPECIFIC** | Depends on Lean/source topology/tooling and is not inherited except for any already-distilled language-neutral law. |
| **OPEN PRESSURE** | Kept visible as a research horizon, not claimed as production support. |

## What was intentionally not copied

Closure does not require copying:

- 154 research files into HRA-N;
- LOAM Observation numbering;
- Lean theorem names or module topology;
- every Alloy/TLA+/DRAKON/D2 artifact;
- completed migration diaries or cutover notes;
- benchmark numbers as HRA-N performance promises;
- historical UI layouts;
- one workflow per historical proof;
- speculative concepts that never earned a production law.

Copying those would weaken the continuity hedge by making HRA-N depend on
LOAM's research archaeology rather than on durable, language-neutral meaning.

## Closure tests

At this checkpoint, research inheritance is considered **sufficiently closed**
when all of the following remain true:

1. **Domain coverage** — major household/accounting research families have an
   explicit HRA-N disposition, including investments, debt, tax, insurance,
   points, reconciliation, FX, inventory and synchronization.
2. **Method coverage** — domain falsification, structural falsification,
   composition-before-retention, D/P/R work allocation, correspondence and
   empirical measurement survive independently of LOAM source layout.
3. **Interaction coverage** — daily-use constraints survive without copying one
   historical UI.
4. **Durability coverage** — escape/export, long-history bounds, recovery,
   migration and retirement remain first-class hedge concerns.
5. **Independence** — a LOAM proof, benchmark, cache, index, or implementation
   structure is never treated as automatic HRA-N evidence.
6. **Traceability** — when detail is needed, the pinned LOAM revision and source
   family can still be consulted.
7. **No frozen parity fiction** — later LOAM research is handled through the
   existing delta-review rhythm rather than pretending this checkpoint freezes
   future work.

These conditions are satisfied for the baseline above by the current HRA-N
design authorities and the additions in this checkpoint.

## What closure does not mean

It does not mean:

- HRA-N implements all researched domains;
- HRA-N and LOAM have identical capabilities;
- the 139 unresolved domain falsification cases are solved;
- every structural pressure has a universal proof;
- current HRA-N persistence or frontend architecture is final;
- future LOAM research can be ignored.

The result is narrower and more useful:

> HRA-N now has a durable map of what LOAM learned, how LOAM learned it, which
> parts must be independently re-qualified in Ada/SPARK, and which historical
> artifacts do not need to be carried forward.

## Reopen triggers

Reopen this closure when:

- LOAM adds a new research family rather than another instance of an existing
  family;
- a new LOAM result changes a distilled HRA-N law;
- HRA-N discovers a counterexample that invalidates this mapping;
- a currently historical/LOAM-specific asset becomes necessary for independent
  long-term operation;
- a domain currently marked only as pressure becomes dogfood-required.

Ordinary LOAM commits continue through `LOAM_ALIGNMENT.md` delta review and do
not require rewriting this matrix unless one of those triggers fires.
